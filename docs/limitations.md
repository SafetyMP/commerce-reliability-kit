# Limitations

This is early, unreleased software. Local synthetic tests do not validate a provider connector, production integration, physical stock, usability, merchant demand, or commercial service. No endorsement or production-readiness seal is offered.

The toolkit relies on caller-declared mappings, account/location scope, completeness, pagination, stock semantics, and explicit evaluation time. Point capture intervals are required for dependent snapshot conclusions. Unknown/non-atomic boundaries, stale evidence, incompatible units, ambiguous identities, and missing coverage remain inconclusive. General bundles, fractional conversion, returns, B2B/EDI, arbitrary transformations, live collectors, and automatic corrections are not implemented.

The six-fixture browser evaluator is narrower than the 24-scenario core. Parity tests cover named overlapping behaviors, not universal equivalence. Reports and drafts are in memory; refresh loses them. A hash identifies only the view. Standalone downloads are supported, but the site is not an offline/PWA application and has no service worker.

Agreement between records does not prove physical stock accuracy. Findings describe supported operational facts; assertion passes can represent correct detection of a problem. Missing evidence does not prove a healthy system. Coverage counts are declarations, not counts of usable atomic evidence.

Demo records are synthetic. Toolkit captures may contain sensitive identifiers and free text after schema filtering. Review exports before sharing; pseudonymization is not anonymity. Pattern scans cannot prove the absence of secrets, customer data, or license issues. See [security](../SECURITY.md).
