# Commercial boundary

Apache-2.0 permits vendors and agencies to embed and commercially reuse the toolkit under its terms. The open toolkit and scenarios are independently useful. Core correctness fixes and contributed scenarios remain open. External participation does not imply endorsement. There is no pay-to-pass program, general reliability certification or production-readiness seal.

A future paid product might provide managed collection, hosting, historical evidence, collaboration, notifications, onboarding and support. None is implemented or required for this standalone local release. No customer accounts, hosted service, monitors, automatic corrections or AI explanations are included.

Developer adoption, merchant demand, partner access and willingness to pay are separate unvalidated hypotheses. Results apply only to named versions, profiles, scenarios, inputs and environments. Fixture passes do not prove vendor compatibility, physical stock accuracy, merchant benefit or commercial demand.
