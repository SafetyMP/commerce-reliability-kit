# Roadmap

The implemented baseline is the CLI/library, 24 synthetic scenarios, normalized imports, reports, and six-fixture static demo. Next priorities are external feedback on evidence semantics, independently reviewed regression scenarios, clearer provider-mapping uncertainty, and documented browser/accessibility acceptance.

Future adapters or broader contracts need scoped proposals, versioned evidence sources, privacy review, and independent oracles before implementation. No adapter catalog, production validation, release date, managed monitoring, or supported provider matrix is promised. Correctness and uncertainty handling take priority over expanding integrations.

The open toolkit and scenarios are independently useful. Future managed collection, hosting, collaboration, notifications, or support would be optional and separate; see the [commercial boundary](COMMERCIAL-BOUNDARY.md) and [governance](../GOVERNANCE.md).
