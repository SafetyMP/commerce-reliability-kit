# Scenario and evidence contract 1.1.0

Author numerical and temporal expectations independently before implementing a rule. Use only synthetic identifiers and quantities. Every fixture supplies purpose, initial conditions, ordered/unordered input declaration, assumptions, capabilities, provenance, explicit evaluation time, evidence, expected findings/non-findings, and assertions. `doesNotEstablish` records limits. Use independently reasoned expectations and retain positive, boundary, and insufficient-evidence cases in public tests.

Declare an inclusive versioned captureInterval on coverage. Public synthetic point captures use start=end=observedAt; missing/null boundaries mean unknown and wider intervals are non-atomic. V0.1 blocks dependent freshness, negative and quantity conclusions in those cases. See IMPORTS.md for boundary validation and migration.

Snapshots are complete state declarations for their resource, account, locations and filter, not event logs with implicit coverage. Every observation must match a coverage snapshot's source, resource and observation timestamp. `sourceRecordId` and observation `id` serve different purposes. Exact repeated observation IDs may deduplicate; conflicting payloads with the same ID are invalid. Shipment/line identity deduplicates repeated delivery across distinct observation IDs. Do not use SKU inference.

All timestamps are UTC ISO strings ending in Z. Source updated/event time orders revisions of the same entity; ingestion time never substitutes. Contradictory revisions at equal source time, unknown revision order, conflicting simultaneous snapshots or mappings yield uncertainty. Future evidence cannot support current conclusions. Normalized evidence/outcome identifier tokens use `[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]+`; `/` is reserved for joining composite targets and diagnostic IDs. Source, order, shipment, line, reversal, mapping and location identifiers therefore cannot collide through embedded slash delimiters. Both portable schemas and runtime validation enforce this restriction. Free-text evidence references can retain original paths. Importers must assign stable, reversible aliases for unsupported raw identifiers and disclose the normalization; simply stripping or replacing slashes can merge distinct records and is not supported.

The earlier pre-release compatibility correction to schema 1.0.0 is retained in 1.1.0: source/entity slashes were already rejected at runtime, and the schemas now consistently reject them in normalized identifier tokens and standalone outcomes. Slash-free fixture identifiers and `SOURCE/ORDER` targets are unchanged. Previously schema-only accepted slash-bearing inputs need explicit normalization. Report evidence uses the same schema definitions. No accepted published release is being migrated.

Coverage statuses are complete/partial/unknown/failed. Only complete, exhausted pagination with known freshness supports absence or exact obligations. This slice supports `filter=all`; arbitrary filtered-scope implications are not evaluated. Completeness remains user/importer-declared. A current complete resource cannot excuse stale evidence for another required resource. Scope changes conservatively block conclusions rather than being merged. Cadence and freshness limit must both be known to establish freshness/staleness.

Order mappings must supply `toRef.locationId` to establish presence/absence in a destination scope. Missing destination location produces INCONCLUSIVE; coverage outside that location cannot establish absence, supply persistence, or prove presence for the mapped order. The initial three order fixtures explicitly map A to A2 at `main`.

Timing boundaries: allowance elapsed at age >= allowance; persistence reached at elapsed >= duration, always with distinct observation instants; stale only at age > freshness limit. All supporting persistence observations must remain fresh at the explicit evaluation time. Partial/failed reads and known presence interrupt order absence. Inventory agreement interrupts mismatch support. Inventory pairs target snapshots with the latest source snapshot at or before the target; a later source snapshot is not retrospectively substituted. An older source instant cannot be reused to satisfy persistence.

Quantities are safe nonnegative integers; absent quantities remain absent. `canceled` means confirmed canceled quantity. Order obligation is ordered minus canceled. Only allocated handed-off/delivered shipment quantities reduce obligation; label generation does not. Reversals and cancellations are independent evidence. A reversal must identify the prior shipment and establish later source time. Surplus on one line does not close a different line; overshipment is reported with magnitude.

Inventory requires mapped items and locations, shared sellable stock scope, declared equivalent net semantics, units and an effective mapping version. Conversion factor is a positive safe integer. Compute converted net, then `max(0, converted - buffer)`; buffer and tolerance are target units. Unsafe arithmetic is inconclusive, never rounded. Unknown semantics, on-hand versus available, overlapping facility groups and unsupported transformations must not be normalized into presumed equivalence.

Meta assertions can expect a diagnostic status, finding presence/absence, or an integration assertion status. Diagnostic assertions additionally accept `expectedReason` and `expectedDetails` (a nonempty object of literal scalar values). Each named detail must exist and equal the expected value; other details are allowed. In demo mode, a missing result is INCONCLUSIVE; mismatched reason, status, missing detail or value is FAIL. In external capture mode, an inconclusive/not-applicable diagnostic cannot contradict a conclusive expectation: the assertion is INCONCLUSIVE. Explicit expected uncertainty may PASS as a meta assertion (or FAIL if its specified reason/details differ), without changing the raw diagnostic. No executable expressions or scenario-specific evaluator branches are used.

Finding assertions optionally accept `expectedEntities`: the exact set of entities across all findings for the named rule. Unexpected entities fail the assertion even if the expected resource also appears. `present: false` asserts there are no findings for that rule anywhere. Line diagnostics use `fulfillment.line:SOURCE/ORDER/LINE`; the order-level `fulfillment.quantities:SOURCE/ORDER` diagnostic remains available. For example, the partial-shipment line oracle literally requires `expectedDetails: {"shipped": 4, "remaining": 6}` for `fulfillment.line:shop/A/L1`.

External completion assertions require both supplied outcomes and sufficient fulfillment evidence. Do not inject fixture outcomes when external evidence is missing. Expected INCONCLUSIVE may pass the meta assertion; it remains INCONCLUSIVE in diagnostic totals and JUnit raw evidence. JUnit testcases represent the selected workflow assertions only; raw diagnostics/integration results and all counts are retained in properties/system-out without changing their statuses.

Changing field or status meaning requires a contract version decision, migration notes, independent oracle review and boundary probes. This slice rejects unknown contract versions rather than silently upgrading them. Keep original source assumptions separate from user policy and derived findings.

## v0.1 optional declarations

Inventory mappings may include `aggregation: {locations: ["east", "west"], nonOverlapping: true}`. These are physical member locations in one complete source snapshot, not guessed aliases or overlapping logical groups. Each member must have explicit quantity, matching unit, equivalent net semantics and stock scope. Sum first, convert, then apply the buffer once. Nonoverlap remains a caller assertion, recorded as an assumption; false/duplicate membership blocks comparison. Missing member positions never become zero. Changes in coverage scope conservatively break support.

Policy may declare `deadlines: [{sourceId: "shop", orderId: "A", at: "2026-10-28T10:10:00Z", milestone: "handed_off"}]`. There is one deadline per source/order. Shipment fields carry explicit `milestoneTime`. At evaluation equal to or after the deadline, compare each line obligation against the qualifying milestone quantity at or before the deadline. Labels never count; delivered time cannot establish an earlier handoff time. Unknown milestone times are inconclusive unless independently sufficient matching evidence already meets the obligation. A current explicit void removes its referenced handoff. Complete fresh order/shipment coverage is required. No business calendar, automatic grace period or historical resurrection of voided evidence is inferred.

The 18 expanded expectations are recorded in [EXPANDED-ORACLES.md](EXPANDED-ORACLES.md). Their generation script contains literal expectations and never calls the evaluator. Unit tests separately exercise mismatch, missing-evidence, temporal and arithmetic boundaries; do not treat passing self-tests as external validation.

## External capture assertion selection

`assert --capture` and library observedEvidence select all diagnostic/finding expectations plus behavioral integrationAssertions. Integration-status meta assertions describe demo behavior and are excluded. Outcomes-only assertion mode remains completion-only. Scenario and behavioral assertion IDs must be distinct. A finding absence expectation needs an applicable diagnostic family with sufficient prerequisites; missing/uncertain/not-applicable diagnostics yield INCONCLUSIVE, including unknown rule IDs. Finding families are order.presence, inventory.available, fulfillment.quantities, fulfillment.deadline and observation.freshness. A detected finding can contradict an absence assertion despite other uncertainty. Exact entity-set expectations also require complete relevant diagnostic evidence. Without declared deadlines there is no applicable deadline diagnostic, so external deadline finding absence is not inferred.

The positive delivery example at examples/order-delivery.scenario.json expects destination_present after the allowance. The existing missing-order demo continues to expect a missing finding. Choose the contract matching the behavior being tested, and keep expectations unchanged between faulty/corrected evidence runs. Computed examples are in examples/capture-integrations.ts.
