# Five tables before the doors open

North Quay Lighting and Mina’s café are fictional. The order, shipment and inventory evidence comes from the kit’s synthetic fixtures. Before opening the file, follow an order from checkout to the packing bench and Jo’s service desk. A customer sees one notice for a carton while the shop may send several cartons for one order. The shift example counts staff time spent tracing inquiries across the desk and packing bench. Its figures illustrate the cost of that work.

Choose **Read Jo’s order file**. The checkout line and the packing slip both come from the shop. Read the quantities before choosing what Jo tells Mina. The resulting customer reply and assignment depend on the response and the coverage of the records. Try another reply to see who owns the follow-up. **Inspect or replay the connector check** shows the assertion and lets you restart the morning decision with different evidence.

At four, Mina opens the carton, counts three lamps herself and asks about two loaners in another finish. Her later count supplies the business context for the loaners. The morning evaluator still sees only its shop records. In **Two loaner lamps for tonight**, three pickups have first claim and eight shoppers try the website before the café arrives. Compare listing three, five or six lamps. The three rows show how many requests each group can fill. Open **Change the stock record** and switch to the count before inspection to discover what remains knowable. A listing is a promise to customers. A count of lamps on the shelf alone cannot establish how many promises the shop can keep.

The support agent decides what to tell this customer, the fulfillment lead can supply independent handoff records, and the integration engineer can reproduce the failure. We propose the ecommerce operations manager as pilot sponsor because the role spans fulfillment and customer service. Shopify describes these responsibilities in its [support roles](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/predefined-roles), [warehouse and fulfillment role](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/role-examples), [fulfillment service developer guide](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/orders-fulfillment/fulfillment-service-apps/build-for-fulfillment-services), and [operations manager overview](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/how-to-overcome-your-daily-operational-frustrations-to-focus-on-future-growth).

For the sponsor, the investment question is whether a shared evidence report saves enough review time to justify preparing and maintaining it. After choosing a listing, switch between ten and fifteen minutes saved to see the first-year value. **Assumptions and calculation** contains the editable inputs and cost comparison. At the illustrative 40 reviews/month, 15 minutes saved, $48/hour and $4,800 first-year cost, net labor capacity is worth $960 and break-even is 12.5 minutes per review. Compare with manual review or adding a quantity check to the connector’s existing test suite. Validate the inputs by measuring comparable tickets, reopened cases and missing records during a four-week pilot on one connector.

Shopify documents total and remaining fulfillment quantities and recommends reconciling webhook-driven state against fetched records. The kit models this problem with synthetic fixtures.
