# Support

Use [repository issues](https://github.com/SafetyMP/commerce-reliability-kit/issues) for sanitized usage questions, reproducible bugs, and feature or scenario proposals. Read the [demo guide](docs/demo-guide.md), [CLI guide](docs/cli-guide.md), and [limitations](docs/limitations.md) first. Bug reports should include scenario, version/revision, environment, reproduction steps, and expected versus observed behavior.

Use the scenario proposal form for evidence or semantic disagreements, and the documentation/usability form for confusing wording or flows. Share minimal synthetic evidence only; never raw customer exports or secrets. Vulnerabilities use the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues. Conduct concerns follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Support is best-effort under [SafetyMP's stewardship](GOVERNANCE.md). No SLA, production monitoring, or commercial support service is currently offered.
