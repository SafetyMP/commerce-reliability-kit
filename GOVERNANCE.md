# Governance and maintenance

GitHub account [SafetyMP](https://github.com/SafetyMP) stewards [Commerce Reliability Kit](https://github.com/SafetyMP/commerce-reliability-kit). No foundation, advisory board, company team, additional maintainers, or independent certification body is represented here. Maintenance and support are best-effort.

The steward owns release integrity, privacy review, attribution, and evidence-contract decisions. Correctness changes require reproducible synthetic evidence, a reasoned oracle separate from evaluator output, executable checks, and review by someone other than the author. If an independent reviewer is unavailable, record that limitation and leave review pending. Record semantic disagreements and resolutions in issues or pull requests with rationale and version implications. Conflicted reviewers should recuse.

Contributors retain copyright and contribute under Apache-2.0. Acceptance depends on evidence and maintainability, not sponsorship. Core correctness fixes and scenarios remain open. Vendors may reuse the toolkit commercially under the license; there is no pay-to-pass program or general reliability certification.

Prioritize correctness and security over feature expansion. Preserve explicit schema/rule versions, compatibility fixtures, and migration notes. Review dependency lockfiles and verify changes. Publication must respect branch checks, required review, and environment approvals. See [deployment and rollback](docs/deployment.md) and [security reporting](SECURITY.md).

If stewardship changes or maintenance stops, document the decision, last supported version, and transfer or archive path. No backup maintainer or separate conduct appeals contact has been established. Results apply only to named versions, policies, inputs, scenarios, and environments; synthetic passes are no provider or production guarantee.
